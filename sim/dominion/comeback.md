# Dominion: comeback and scorcher experiments

Generated by `node --import tsx sim/dominion/comeback.ts --sweep 100 --seasons 200`. Defaults: terraformStep 0.4, lateJoinGrant 40. Sweep rows use 100 seasons on one shared seed set (separate from the main report's seeds); the final table re-runs the picks over the main 200 seasons.

Goal: bring the leader-at-25% win rate down from the defaults (48% on the main seeds; 25% would be random) without letting any strategy pass 40%, and lift the scorcher, which wins least.

## New knobs (off by default)

- `upkeepScaleExp` with `upkeepScaleRef` 6: above 6 worlds, every world's upkeep is multiplied by (worlds / 6)^exp. Big empires pay more per world.
- `leaderUpkeepMult`: the current leader (bank + 24 ticks of yield) pays this much extra upkeep.
- `edgeLateYield`: edge-ring output rises linearly over the season to (1 + this) at the end, a late frontier boom for whoever is out there.
