# Dominion API balance report

Generated by `node --import tsx sim/dominion/run.ts --seasons 200 --sweep 100`. 200 seasons of 168 ticks, four bots (one per strategy), 10 maps of 40-80 worlds, random start rings, and a late starter in 79 seasons. Everything is deterministic: the same command gives the same report.

## The two questions

### 1. Does one strategy always win?

**Not always, but one strategy is clearly favoured: the terraformer wins 50% of 200 seasons** (an even game would be 25%; margin about +-7%). Every strategy wins some seasons: scorcher 11%, terraformer 50%, prospector 20%, raider 20%.

Winner spread (normalized entropy, 1.00 = all four equally often): 0.89. The top strategy differs between maps: 4 different strategies are the most frequent winner on at least one of the 10 maps.

### 2. Does the early leader always stay ahead?

**No.** The leader at 25% of the season finishes first in 35% of seasons (the leader at 50% in 48%). With four players a random leader would hold on 25% of the time.

### And can a late starter at the edge still finish well?

**Not at the defaults.** Late starters (79 seasons; they join 20-30% into the season at the edge) finish with a mean rank of 3.65 of 4, in the top two 9% of the time, and win 2 times.

## Dominance and the fix

The terraformer wins more than 40% of seasons at the defaults, so the knobs aimed at that strategy were tried (100 seasons each, same seeds):

| change | scorcher | terraformer | prospector | raider | entropy | leader@25% wins | balance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| defaults (same seeds) | 9% | 59% | 20% | 12% | 0.80 | 36% | 0.47 |
| terraformStep 0.6 -> 0.4 | 11% | 40% | 32% | 17% | 0.92 | 46% | 0.65 |
| terraformCost 30 -> 45 | 10% | 42% | 34% | 14% | 0.89 | 42% | 0.63 |
| terraformCap 3 -> 2 | 9% | 53% | 24% | 14% | 0.84 | 39% | 0.54 |

Best single change: **terraformStep 0.6 -> 0.4**. Re-run over the full 200 seasons (the main seeds):

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| terraformStep 0.6 -> 0.4 | 15% | 33% | 28% | 24% | 0.97 | 47% | 3.72 |

## Reading the results

- With `bandwidthExponent` = 0.7 the scorcher wins 92% and the leader at 25% wins 89%: without the square-root curve, the biggest empire simply out-acts everyone. The sqrt rule is doing real work.
- With `bandwidthExponent` = 1 the scorcher wins 91% and the leader at 25% wins 93%: without the square-root curve, the biggest empire simply out-acts everyone. The sqrt rule is doing real work.
- The terraformer is the strategy to tune. `terraformStep 0.6 -> 0.4` brings it from 50% to 33% of wins.
- Weakening it raises the snowball rate (leader@25% wins 35% -> 47%), because the terraformer's late surge was what overturned early leaders. That is still well short of "always", but it means the comeback mechanic needs a second source once the terraformer is tamed: `leaderWeight` alone did not provide it in the sweep.
- The newcomer floor and the cheap edge are not enough for a late starter: mean rank 3.65 of 4. A catch-up grant helps (`lateJoinGrant` = 40 gives a mean rank of 2.62 in the sweep) without changing which strategies win.

## Caveats

- The bots are simple first-week scripts. A strategy that loses here may only be badly played; a strategy that wins may be exploiting a bot that does not defend well. Treat the numbers as a first read on the rules, not a verdict on human play.
- Every season has exactly one player per strategy, so "win rate" means "wins against one of each other strategy".
- The score is resources in the bank at the end of the season. A different victory rule (held worlds, a mix) would change which strategy is strongest.
