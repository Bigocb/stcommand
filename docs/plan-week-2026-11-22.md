## 3. Lessons from the week (new ones; the 11-18 and 11-19 entries are already in `docs/lessons-learned.md`)

1. **Hull count is the profit lever once routes are good, and we bought late.** Rate tracked the trading fleet (traders selling
   at once 14 -> 29 -> 36; 0.8 -> 5.8 -> 11.1M/h). From 22:12 on 11-21 every hour had 0 free traders while the board listed 450-600k
   routes nobody flew. Rule: when free traders are 0 for two hourlies and the wallet clears the working-capital floor plus a hull,
   propose a hull that hour. This is what §4 automates.
2. **Releases must track traders, not a fixed target.** 29 of 35 recent releases unsold; each staged system costs about 8 probes
   and API reads. Rule: `freshTarget` steps with big-hull count (§2.1).
3. **Deploys cost trading time; batch them.** 11-21 21:40-22:05: deploys at 21:40, 21:55, 22:00 and a heap OOM at 22:04; the
   21:13-22:12 hour made 7.66M against 10-17M either side. Operator rule (11-21): batch code into one deploy; new behaviour ships
   behind a clause that is off, so switching it on needs no restart.
4. **The engine's working set grew with the galaxy until it fell over.** SEV0 at 05:30 on 11-21 (route build 28 s, process 95%
   busy) and the heap OOM at 22:04 had one cause: an all-pairs price join (1.38M rows) and full-ship `/api/state` bodies.
   Rule: anything that scales with live systems or ships gets a cap (top-N, cache, count-change) before it ships; watch
   `ops container` and the route-build time in the hourly once the fleet passes ~150 live systems.
5. **The route list overstates a new hull's value 2-3x.** Wave 2 was bought for 450-615k routes (list, at 225); the dispatcher
   gave the ships 155-379k trips elsewhere. The list prices at the biggest hold, and (02:30 finding, #73) the dispatcher offers only one
   buy market per good and sell market, so the board's "unflown" alternates are never assigned. Rule: size hull buys on about
   300k/h per hull, and pick yards from the dispatcher's work items, not `unflown_routes`, until #73 is fixed.
6. **A long-running process still stalls.** 11-16 23:10: trading stopped for 1.5 h at about 31 h uptime (same as 11-10). Now
   the stall tile and push exist; keep a planned restart before ~28 h uptime if no deploy happens.

## 4. Project for the week: the engine buys its own trading hulls

### 4.1 What exists

- `FleetManager.maybeBuyShip()` (`src/engine/fleet.ts` ~4486): a ladder (tour shuttle if none, miners to `minerTarget`, explorer,
  then a scored hull) behind `autoBuyShips` (off since October), capped by `shipBudget` (max 500k) and `shipCap:*` (4 per frame).
  It cannot buy a heavy or bulk freighter and knows nothing of routes, payback or the reset.
- `src/engine/buyAdvice.ts` (pure): the operator's cash rules as warnings (working-capital floor, 3M line, big-hull line 3.5M plus
  1M in holds, last big hull not paid back in 6 h, bad economy finding). The buy tools and the Deck/Tower button show it.
- `ops unflown_routes`: routes on the board nobody flies, with the nearest free trader and the nearest big-hull yards (price, hops).
- The approvals gate (`decide_approval`, pre-approved opening tasks).

### 4.2 What it should do (the operator's standing rules, from CLAUDE.md, as the spec)

1. **Need:** buy only when traders are the constraint: no free trader for N minutes (default 30) and at least K unflown routes
   (default 3) worth at least X at the candidate's hold (default 200k at 225), with fresh prices (under 30 min) on both ends.
2. **Cash:** wallet after the buy above `workingCapitalFloor()` (big hulls x 400k + others x 100k) plus the 3M line; big hulls need
   3.5M and 1M in holds; bulk only over 15M (and only where markets take a 490 load, see 5).
3. **Payback:** the last big hull must have earned back its price in matched profit, or the wallet must be over Y (say 25M);
   expected earnings before the reset (hours left x the fleet's measured per-hull rate) must exceed the price by a margin
   (default 1.5x), which also ends buying near the reset without a fixed cutoff.
4. **Where:** the yard nearest the route the hull is meant for (CLAUDE.md, "Buying a ship"): `price + hops x 7k`, a yard in the
   target system preferred; a refining freighter only when its yard is in the trading system; a yard sells only to a docked ship.
5. **Which hull:** heavy (225) by default. Bulk (490) only when the target routes' markets take a 490 load (trade volume x lots),
   since busy markets stop a load near 225 (THEO-A28 stopped at 225, margin -23c on the next lot).
6. **Pace:** at most one big hull per M minutes (default 30) and one per target system; never into falling margins (no release
   for 3 h and profit per sale falling: lessons 11-18).
7. **Swaps:** once bigger hulls are affordable, propose retiring the worst 40/80 hull after its replacement trades (operator rule
   11-20), as a separate step.

### 4.3 Build, as a self-contained module (CLAUDE.md "Build new logic as self-contained modules")

- `src/engine/hullBuyer.ts`, pure: `decideHullBuy(input, policy) -> { buy?: {shipType, yard, reason, expected}, wait?: reason }`.
  Input: wallet, holds, floor, big-hull count, free traders and minutes since one was free, the unflown routes (value at the
  candidate hold, price ages, buy system), yard offers with hops, last big hull (price, matched since bought), hours to reset,
  measured per-hull rate, economy findings. Every rule above is a policy value backed by a doctrine clause.
- Clause `hullAutoBuy` with three modes, shipping **off**: 1 = **shadow** (log "would buy X at Y because ..." and show it in the
  hourly and on Tower's Approvals, buy nothing); 2 = **card** (raise an approval card with the reason and the numbers; the
  operator's tap buys); 3 = **auto** (buy within a per-day budget clause, then set role trader).
- State it decides from (last buy time, the hull it is waiting to pay back) is saved durably (fleet flag) and restored at boot
  (operator rule 11-19), with a test that a fresh instance restores it.
- Tests: the rules one by one (each off reproduces "no buy"), the 11-17 thin-wallet case (must refuse), Saturday night's case
  (must propose: 0 free traders, 200M wallet, 450-600k routes), the near-reset case (must refuse when hours left x rate < 1.5 x
  price), and the seam where `FleetManager` calls it.
- Measure: every proposal and buy is an `action_events` row with its expected rate; a `hull_payback` read in ops (hours to repay,
  realized vs expected) so the defaults can be tuned from data, not guessed.
